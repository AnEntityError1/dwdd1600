Helldiver Factions

Super Earth

The Federation of Super Earth is the governing faction of Super Earth and its territory. They are at war against three intergalactic alien factions: The Automatons, the Terminids, and the Illuminate. Super Earth is managed by nine governmental ministries:

  • The Ministry of Defense
  • The Ministry of Expansion
  • The Ministry of Humanity
  • The Ministry of Prosperity
  • The Ministry of Science
  • The Ministry of Truth
  • The Ministry of Unity
  • The Ministry of Employment
  • The Ministry of Intelligence

The Automatons

The Automatons - colloquially known as Bots - are intelligent robotic beings created by the Cyborgs before the events of the Second Galactic War. They are fully capable of feeling emotions - such as sorrow and anger - and having independent thoughts. They predominantly utilize fusion weapons, rockets, and melee weapons. The Automatons currently consist of their base units and three sub factions:

  1. The Jet Brigade
  2. The Incineration Corps
  3. The Cyborg Legion

The Terminids

The Terminids, insect-like creatures referred to as Bugs before their scientific name was given, are rapidly-reproducing, swarming aliens. Much of the Terminids attack using their claws and acidic bile. They now swarm the planets north-east of the Sol System. The Terminids currently exist as their base strain and three sub factions:

  1. The Predator Strain
  2. The Spore Burst Strain
  3. The Rupture Strain

The Illuminate

The Illuminate - colloquially known as Squids - are an ancient hierarchical alien species named Squ'ith who were originally aquatic in nature. Few in number, they possess exceptionally long lifespans (upwards of a thousand years) and highly advanced technology - such as stealth and shielding capabilities. The Illuminate currently exist as their base selves and four sub factions:

  1. The Mindless Masses
  2. The Appropriators
  3. The Vote Snatchers
  4. The Invasion Fleet
