The Federation of Super Earth

What is Super Earth

Super Earth is a Managed Democracy, where elections are preformed via computer aided voting software. This software asks citizens several questions, the answers of which are used to algorithmically select a candidate that would best match their beliefs. This removed "the uncertainty that existed in the old systems where voters didn't understand fully what they were voting for". Each Citizen is separated into one of 6 known classes (Class A to Class F) by the Citizenship Classification Structure, or CCS.

The Government of Super Earth is seperated into multiple different but intercommunicating portions, including- but not limitid to - The Ministries.

The Ministries

The Ministry of Defense
The Ministry of Defense is led by the Minister of Defense, who is first in the Super Earth presidential line of succession.
The Ministry of Defense commands the Super Earth Armed Forces, and by extension The Helldivers.
- - -
The Ministry of Expansion
The Ministry of Expansion tirelessly works to expand the reach of Freedom and secures vital resources for our society to thrive.
- - -
The Ministry of Humanity
The Ministry of Humanity maintains healthy, productive voters by managing education, healthcare, social grading, and gene management.
- - -
The Ministry of Prosperity
The Ministry of Prosperity manages Super Earth's economy and ensures a steady flow of resources to the most deserving
- - -
The Ministry of Science
The Ministry of Science is responsible for continuous scientific progress, augmenting our moral superiority over our enemies with technological superiority.
- - -
The Ministry of Truth
The Ministry of Truth is the sole provider of information to the public. All posters speaking about the other ministers are created and distributed by the Ministry of Truth.
- - -
The Ministry of Unity
The Ministry of Unity ensures that all of mankind is united as one by protecting us from internal threats, such as deviant behavior and expression of dangerous ideas.
- - -
The Ministry of Employment
The Ministry of Employment is only mentioned within the Helldiver Contract of Employment. There is no other known reference to the Ministry.
- - -
The Ministry of Intelligence
The Ministry of Intelligence seems to be a military intelligence agency within Super Earth, but little else is known.
- - -

The Super Earth Armed Forces (S.E.A.F.)

The SEAF - Super Earth Armed Forces - are the military of the Federation of Super Earth, managed and commanded by the Ministry of Defense. The SEAF is split into different branches: the Super Earth Army, the Super Earth Navy, and of course the elite Helldivers, who additionally receive Major Orders from Super Earth High Command.

While the Helldivers are the scalpel of the military might of Super Earth, the regular Army Corps are its hammer, responsible for the bulk of the fighting in the Galactic War, as well as taking over more permanent security duties over liberated planets. While the exact strength of the Army Corps is highly classified, the Army is likely the largest of the branches in sheer numbers, as they can hold multiple fronts on dozens of planets - something that likely requires vast reserves of manpower numbering in the tens of billions.
