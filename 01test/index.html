Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet, technology, and digital devices in a safe, responsible, respectful, and ethical way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, computers, and the internet.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules and guidelines that explains how people are allowed to use an organization's technology, computers, internet, and digital resources.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, or work and present them as your own without giving proper credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is when a student cheats or uses dishonest methods to gain an unfair advantage in schoolwork or exams.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

AI-generated content should be used responsibly, honestly, and according to your school or teacher's rules.
